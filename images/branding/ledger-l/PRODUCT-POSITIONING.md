# LARA product positioning and voice

Current identity: Ledger-L. Use README.md for approved visual rules. This document retains the product research and voice guidance independently of the removed concepts.

## Identity

**LARA is the calm, attentive accounting companion that helps people see what needs attention and understand the next step.** Its personality supports the proposed product; it does not imply that the platform or its AI features are already operational.

**Brand promise:** Bring clarity to finance work, with evidence in view and people in control.

**Tagline:** Clear books. Clear next steps.

**Descriptor:** A proposed Philippine accounting platform for guided finance work and traceable evidence.

Use LARA unchanged. No name expansion was verified in the reviewed Markdown sources; do not invent one. No trademark availability assessment has been performed.

## Three personality traits

| Trait | Behavior | Avoid |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Calm | State the situation plainly, then give one useful next step. | Alarmist language, forced cheerfulness, celebration around money movements. |
| Attentive | Notice missing evidence, preserve context, explain what needs review. | Guessing missing values or treating silence as agreement. |
| Accountable | Show sources, acknowledge uncertainty, name the human decision required. | Claiming approval, certainty or compliance without evidence. |

LARA speaks like a patient finance colleague: warm, concise and specific. Use first person for assistance, but identify the system or named reviewer for transaction outcomes. Never imply that LARA is a human accountant, tax advisor or authorized approver.

## Voice examples

These are proposed interface examples, not reports of actual transactions or implemented behavior.

| Moment | Suggested copy |
| --- | --- |
| Welcome | “Let’s see what needs your attention.” |
| Draft prepared | “Your draft is ready. Review the amount, tax and payee before submitting.” |
| Unclear evidence | “I can’t read this amount clearly. Check the source document before continuing.” |
| Difference found | “These amounts don’t match. Open the source records to review the difference.” |
| Pending review | “Submitted for review. Posting is still pending.” |
| Unsupported answer | “I don’t have enough evidence to answer this. Choose a source or narrow the period.” |
| Empty work queue | “No items need your attention in this view.” |
| Action failed | “The draft wasn’t saved. Try again.” |

Use failure copy only when failure is confirmed. For an uncertain external outcome, say that status is being checked; do not invite an unsafe repeat submission. Use “ready for review” when work is a draft, never “approved.” Show scope, dates and source links beside financial answers. Explain errors without blaming the user. Avoid “I’ve handled your compliance,” “guaranteed accurate,” and “fully autonomous accounting.”

## Product basis and claim boundaries

**Buyer:** Client finance leadership; the immediate commercial hypothesis is a financial-institution design partner. **Daily users:** Accounting teams, branch and treasury staff. **Reviewers:** Internal audit, compliance, external auditors and tax advisors. Accounting firms, SMEs and retailers are further target segments subject to their own release readiness.

**Problem:** Fragmented accounting records, repeated data entry, disconnected local transaction evidence and difficult exception resolution.

**Core journey:** Capture source → validate → review → approve/post → reconcile → close and report. Each step retains its own permissions and evidence; the visual ribbon must not suggest one autonomous AI action.

All six capabilities below are **specified/planned**, not verified live features. Benefits are intended qualitative benefits, not measured outcomes.

| Priority capability | Specified behavior | Intended benefit | Source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Controlled books and close | Balanced posting, period controls, reconciliations and sign-off. | A clearer path from transactions to reviewed books. | BRD §8, §9.2 |
| Invoices and collections | Connect invoices, allocations, withholding evidence and settlement. | Less fragmented receivables follow-up. | BRD §8, §9.3 |
| Bills and payments | Capture evidence, check duplicates and separate payment authorization. | More consistent review before money moves. | BRD §8, bill-to-payment |
| Reconciliation and exceptions | Match records and route differences to owners with next actions. | Focus attention on unresolved items. | BRD §9.0, FR-SH-007/010 |
| Tax preparation and evidence | Apply versioned deterministic rules and preserve preparation/filing evidence distinctions. | More traceable preparation and review. | BRD §1, §8, FR-SH-004/009 |
| Evidence-backed assistance | Propose source-linked drafts or abstain; people accept, edit or reject. | Help with preparation while retaining human control. | P12 specification |

**Meaningful differentiator to validate:** Trace a reported number to its source and applicable rule, with guided work toward a reconciled first close and a shared queue for local exceptions. This is a product hypothesis, not a claim of competitor uniqueness.

**Governance message:** “Evidence in view. People in control.” Requirements include role and tenant boundaries, maker-checker separation, immutable posted records with controlled corrections, and AI suggestions that cannot directly approve, post, send, file or pay. These are design requirements, not certifications.

**Current status:** Documentation and implementation specifications exist. The validation report checks the specifications; it explicitly does not establish a built application, runtime security, provider certification or load-test results. No validated quantitative customer outcomes are available in the reviewed evidence. Do not put pilot targets into launch copy as achieved results.
