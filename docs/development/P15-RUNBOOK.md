# P15 intercompany and consolidation runbook

Extends `P02-RUNBOOK.md` through `P14-RUNBOOK.md`; earlier procedures apply unchanged. Consolidation reads approved member statement snapshots and writes only the group's own reporting book (`CONSOL`, a management book in the reporting entity); it never posts to, corrects or rewrites a member ledger. An intercompany pair is two locally authorized transactions: each side is prepared, approved and posted under its own entity's authority and maker-checker.

## Activation gates

The reporting entity activates `group_accounting` (after `multi_currency` and `assets`) with evidence and an independent approval. The group accountant defines the group (`/group`, `POST /groups`): name, reporting currency and wholly-owned members under the full method — the reporting entity is itself a member. Partial ownership or the equity method is refused until an approved extension exists (`FEATURE_NOT_ENABLED`). A mapping version is drafted from the member charts (`POST /groups/{id}/mappings`) and approved by a second principal (`POST /group-mappings/{id}/approve`, `group.activate`); the reviewer then activates the group with a reason. Rate sets (`POST /rate-sets`: closing, average and historical per member currency for one period end, from the source publication) are approved by a second principal (`consolidation.approve`). Approved mappings and rate sets are immutable; corrections are new versions or codes.

## Intercompany pairs

The source entity raises a pair (`/group/pairs`, `POST /intercompany-pairs`) on an invoice or bill with the target draft the counterparty entity will accept; both entities must be members of one active group and both sides carry the source currency. The target entity accepts (`POST /intercompany-pairs/{id}/accept`, `intercompany_pair.accept`): the draft becomes that entity's own document with the accepter as preparer, and its submission, approval and posting follow the entity's normal maker-checker — the accepter never approves it. A gross that differs between the sides marks the pair `exception` at once. Each side posts with `POST /intercompany-pairs/{id}/post` from its own entity; a side that cannot post (unapproved, period locked, missing rate) leaves the pair in `exception` with the reason, the other side stays exactly as it was, and nothing is fabricated. Resolve the cause in the entity concerned and post again; the pair completes when both sides are posted. A refused pair (`decision: reject`) and a posted pair are final.

## Running a consolidation

Freeze each member's close for the period end (soft-close or lock the period) and generate a `statements` snapshot per member; `/group/consolidations` shows readiness per member (period status, latest snapshot, rate coverage). Create the run (`POST /consolidations`: the member snapshot ids, an approved rate set for the period end, an approved mapping version) and preview it (`POST /consolidations/{id}/preview`). The worksheet (`GET /consolidations/{id}/worksheet`) shows each member's balances with the rate and policy applied (balance sheet at closing, income at the approved period average, equity and prior earnings at historical), the translation difference per member booked to the group's translation reserve line, the eliminations derived from pairs posted on both sides (the reciprocal receivable and payable still outstanding at the cutoff; intra-group revenue and expense of the period, each once), manual eliminations with reason and evidence, the consolidated statements with member and elimination columns, and every unresolved difference (sides that differ in the transaction currency, unmapped accounts, pairs not posted on both sides). Differences are shown, never plugged: the reviewer cannot approve while any remains. The same snapshots, mapping and rates yield the same result hash; a new snapshot is a new run version. Approval (`consolidation.approve`, a principal other than the preparer) freezes the inputs and result; publication (`consolidation.publish`) writes the `consolidated_statements` report snapshot in the group book with the result hash as checksum and supersedes the earlier published run for the period.

## Rollback

Disable the capability in the reporting entity: runs stop, published snapshots remain readable and every member ledger is unchanged. A wrong published run is superseded by a corrected run for the same period, never deleted. Pairs in `exception` are resolved in the entity concerned; an issued side is never reversed by the pair — a correction is that entity's own correcting document.

## Evidence to preserve

For every consolidation incident: the run id and result hash, the member snapshot ids and checksums, the mapping version hash, the rate set and its source evidence, the elimination entries with pair references, the translation adjustments with their rates, and the pair audit ranges in both entities. Export them with `POST /exports` in the reporting entity and in each member before any operator action.
