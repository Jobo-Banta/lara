# P05 purchasing, payables and expenses runbook

Extends `P02-RUNBOOK.md`, `P03-RUNBOOK.md` and `P04-RUNBOOK.md`; the workspace, ledger and sales procedures apply unchanged. Direct edits to posted `lara.documents`, `lara.receipts_of_service`, reviewed `lara.beneficiary_versions`, released or settled `lara.payment_orders`, `lara.advances`, `lara.advance_events`, `lara.tax_events` or issued `lara.withholding_certificates` are prohibited; the database refuses them for every application role, and a privileged edit is a Sev1 posting-integrity incident handled as in the P03 runbook. No screen or operation in this release sends money: release and settlement record what happened at the bank, with evidence.

## Activation gates

Purchasing work on an entity needs, in order: the `sales` capability active, the `purchasing` capability activated with evidence and an independent approval (`/settings/capabilities`), and an approved `purchasing_profile` settings version naming the payables control account, input tax account, cash account, withholding payable account (when any supplier withholds), employee advance account (when advances or claims are used), `withholdingRecognition` (`accrual` or `payment`, signed off by finance per the customer profile), `requirePurchaseOrder` with the `nonPoAccountIds` that utility and similar bills may use without an order, `requireReceiptOfService`, `duplicateWindowDays`, `expensePolicyVersion` and `supplierWithholding` (supplier id → active withholding rule code). A numbering series of kind `bill` per branch is optional: without one, bills post unnumbered and are identified by the supplier reference. Until the reviewed contract gains operations for the profile, series and beneficiaries, an operator seeds `lara.settings_versions` (kind `purchasing_profile`, status `approved`, author and approver different), `lara.document_series` and `lara.beneficiary_versions` through the runtime role with the tenant set, exactly as `tests/browser/workspace.spec.mjs` does. Never approve a beneficiary version without the bank verification document in evidence.

## Duplicate supplier references

An exact duplicate (`DUPLICATE_SOURCE`) is a bill for the same supplier whose reference, ignoring case, spacing and punctuation, is already recorded and not cancelled; the original keeps its reference and the new entry must be cancelled or re-referenced. A possible duplicate (same supplier, same gross, document date within `duplicateWindowDays`, different reference) opens a `duplicate_review` task at submission; the approver must record the disposition in the approval reason, which resolves the task. Response to a confirmed duplicate found after posting: correct the wrong bill with a full `reversal` supplier credit (`POST /bills/{id}/correct`), never by editing or deleting; record the finding on the task.

## Two-way match differences

A PO-linked bill is refused (`STATE_CONFLICT`, message naming ordered, billed and excess) when the net billed across all non-cancelled bills would exceed the ordered net, or, under the receipt-of-service policy, the accepted total. The orders screen shows ordered, billed and difference per order. Resolution: record the receipt of service with its evidence (domain `recordReceipt`; no contract operation yet), amend the order before approval (approved orders are not edited; cancel and re-raise when nothing is billed), or bill the excess as a non-PO bill on a non-PO account when policy allows. Orders with bills never cancel.

## Withholding timing

The profile chooses when supplier withholding is recognized. Under `accrual` the bill posts `Cr withholding payable` and a header `tax_event` (recognition `accrual`); the payable opens net of withholding and payments carry zero withholding. Under `payment` the bill opens gross; every payment proposal must carry the proportional withholding of the bills it settles (`VALIDATION_FAILED` states the expected amount), the settlement posts `Cr withholding payable` and records one `payment` event per bill share. Changing the profile only affects bills posted afterwards; a bill keeps its `withholding_recognition`. A returned payment reverses its withholding events (mirror event, original marked `reversed_by`); certificates exclude both. Certificates (`prepareCertificate`, review and issue by different principals) sum live header events for a supplier, rule and period, subtract supplier credits and carry a checksum; re-issue as a new version after any change, never edit an issued one.

## Payment authority incidents

Authority (`POST /payments/{id}/authorize`) is refused to whoever prepared or submitted the proposal or the order, binds the proposal's content version and approves the proposal. Any edit to the proposal or a newly approved beneficiary version returns authorized or submitted orders to draft (audit action `payment.authority_withdrawn`) and the proposal to draft; the release recheck refuses a stale authority with `STATE_CONFLICT`. Response: resubmit the proposal and the order and obtain authority again; never reuse an old release reference. Release records the manual channel, the bank reference and evidence (bank-file and API channels arrive with P06); it posts nothing. Settlement (`POST /payments/{id}/settle`) is the single posting and is idempotent under the same key or a repeated command on a settled order. A bank return after settlement is recorded with `POST /payments/{id}/return` (mirrored entry, allocations unwound, payable reopened); an issued employee advance is never returned this way — the employee's return is a receipt.

## Advances and claims

An employee advance is a payment proposal to an employee with no allocations; settlement posts `Dr employee advances / Cr cash` and creates the advance. A claim records the employee's oldest open advance at creation and, when it posts, liquidates it up to its remaining balance (`Cr employee advances`), opening a payable to the employee only for the difference. The unused remainder comes back as a receipt (`/collections`, party = employee, no allocations), which posts `Dr cash / Cr employee advances` and records `return` events FIFO across the employee's open advances; the database refuses liquidations or returns beyond the balance and a second liquidation by the same claim. `listAdvances` shows amount, used, remaining and state per advance; a non-zero employee advances balance with no open advances is an incident.

## Reports

Supplier aging runs as `POST /reports` with `reportType=ap_aging` into an immutable snapshot (JSON or CSV stored as restricted evidence). Open payables and statements come from `GET /open-items?side=AP`, whose amounts derive from allocation events at read time; payables are net of accrued withholding.

## Evidence to preserve

For every purchasing incident: trace ids, the `audit_events` range for the entity, the document ids and supplier references, `receipts_of_service` ids, the settlement and payment order ids with their release and settlement references and evidence ids, the beneficiary version ids, the journal entry ids, the allocation and advance event ids, the tax event ids and the certificate ids. Export them with `POST /exports` before any operator action.
