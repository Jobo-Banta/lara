# Branch review corrections — 21 September 2026

A defect review of `feat/p02-contracts-migrations` (PR #5, P02–P18) after the last implementation ticket was recorded done. Every finding below was verified against the source before it was changed; each fix carries a regression case in the suite named, and migration `0034_review_corrections.sql` holds the schema-level changes. Nothing here changes a release boundary: the modules stay behind their capabilities and the release records stay drafts.

## Noted for the owner (not changed)

- `acceptInvite` compares an email the caller asserts in the body (the BFF identity carries no email claim); possession of the 48-hex token delivered to the mailbox is the actual proof. Binding the accepted identity to a verified email claim needs the OIDC session to carry one.
- The public verify and webhook rate limit is keyed on the socket address, which is the proxy's behind the loopback supervisor; per-client limiting needs a trusted-proxy header decision.
- Cash sessions: two cashiers of one branch on one business date share the branch's cash in their expected floats; attributing cash to a cashier needs a drawer or session reference on the settlement.
- `inventory.recostPreviewFor` treats `transfer` movements of the warehouse as issues when replaying later movements; inbound transfers should be receipts of the target warehouse — confirm the intended replay before P10 release.
