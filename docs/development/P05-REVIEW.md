# P05 implementation review — 20 September 2026

P05 is in progress, not released. Build-order steps 1 (contracts, migrations, permission and capability definitions), 2 (domain and accounting rules), 3 (API and jobs), 4 (user journeys) and 5 (acceptance and operations) are implemented; no `purchasing` capability is activated for a live tenant, no beneficiary is approved anywhere and no payment has been released outside a test tenant.

## P05-01 contracts and migrations

Contracts: the 36 P05 operations (`/bills` create/list/get/edit/submit/approve/post/correct, `/purchase-orders` create/list/get/edit/submit/approve/cancel, `/expense-claims` create/list/get/edit/submit/approve/post, `/settlements` create/list/get/edit/submit, `/payments` create/list/get/edit/submit/authorize/release/settle/return) and their schemas (`DocumentCreate/Resource/Correction`, `SettlementCreate/Resource`, `PaymentCreate/Resource/Release`, `SettlementEvidence`, `Reversal`) were already in the reviewed OpenAPI and are compiled by `@lara/contracts`. The 31 P05 permissions (`bill.*`, `purchase_order.*`, `expense_claim.*`, `settlements.*`, `payment.*`) and the `purchasing` capability (depends on `sales`) were seeded by migration `0009`; no contract or seed change was needed.

Migration `0019_p05_purchasing_schema.sql` adds the purchasing facts around the shared document, settlement and open-item tables:

- `documents` gains `supplier_reference_normalized` (unique per supplier while the bill is not cancelled — the duplicate key of the specification), `withholding` bounded by the net and backed by `withholding_rule_version_id`, and `withholding_recognition`; posted immutability covers them.
- `receipts_of_service` (order, evidence, accepting principal, lines, total; append-only), `beneficiary_versions` (encrypted account number, last four digits, reviewer distinct from author, one approved version per party, immutable once reviewed, draft → approved | rejected → superseded), `payment_orders` (draft → submitted → authorized → released | release_unknown → settled | failed | returned; authority binds `authorized_settlement_version` and the beneficiary; release and settlement each need channel/reference/evidence and are immutable; one live order per settlement), `advances` with append-only `advance_events` (liquidation once per claim, returns; the guard refuses unposted settlements and amounts beyond the balance; status derives from events), `expense_claims` (employee, advance, policy version) and `withholding_certificates` (derived events, reviewed output version, checksum; draft → reviewed → issued → superseded, immutable once issued).
- `0020_p05_supplier_credit_allocations.sql`: a posted credit note whose source is a bill allocates on the payable side up to its gross net of withholding, and settled payment orders may be returned.

Verification: `scripts/test-p05-schema.mjs` (9 groups) in CI on fresh and upgraded databases; `scripts/snapshot-lib.mjs` and `scripts/tenant-teardown.mjs` cover the seven new tables.

## P05-02 domain and accounting rules

`packages/domain/src/purchasing.mjs` builds on the P04 kernel (`sales.mjs` now exports its document primitives: rule resolution, `computeLines`, line writing, numbering, allocation events, settlement resources) and adds:

- Purchasing profile (`settings_versions` kind `purchasing_profile`, approved): payables, input tax, cash, withholding payable and employee advance accounts; `withholdingRecognition` `accrual` (default) or `payment`; `requirePurchaseOrder` with `nonPoAccountIds` (utility bills need no dummy order); `requireReceiptOfService`; `duplicateWindowDays`; `expensePolicyVersion`; `supplierWithholding` mapping supplier ids to active withholding rule codes (the party contract has no withholding field yet).
- Bills: supplier role, mandatory supplier reference normalized (lower case, letters and digits only); an exact duplicate is `DUPLICATE_SOURCE` and the original reference is retained (the unique index catches the concurrent race and translates to the same code); the same gross within the window under a different reference opens a `duplicate_review` task at submission, and approval then requires the reviewer's disposition (`reason`), which resolves the task. Lines post to expense or asset accounts (never control or revenue accounts). Evidence is mandatory before review for bills, supplier credits and claims.
- Two-way match: a PO-linked bill (source `purchase_order`, relation `order`) is checked at creation, edit and posting against the ordered net across all non-cancelled linked bills and, under the receipt-of-service policy, against the accepted total from `receipts_of_service` (`recordReceipt`, evidence required, accepting principal recorded). Orders with bills do not cancel.
- Posting (AC-03/04): `Dr expense (per account and dimensions) / Dr input tax / Cr payables (gross − accrued withholding) / Cr withholding payable`; input tax events per line; a header withholding event with recognition `accrual`; the payable open item is the residual after recognized withholding. Under the payment-time profile the bill carries no withholding event and the full gross opens; the settlement recognizes the proportional share once (`tax_events` with recognition `payment`, one per bill share) and posts `Cr withholding payable`. A payment against an accrual-recognized bill must carry zero withholding (`VALIDATION_FAILED` names the expected amount), so tax is never repeated.
- Supplier credits (`correctDocument`: `credit_note`, `reversal`, `additional_invoice`) mirror the bill within the remaining creditable net per account, apply to the bill's payable through the guarded allocation path and reduce the certificate basis.
- Expense claims name an employee, record the policy version and the oldest open advance at creation; posting liquidates the advance up to its remaining balance (`Cr employee advances`) and opens a payable to the employee for the difference. The unused remainder is returned as a receipt from the employee through `/collections`: `sales.postCollection` treats a receipt from a party that is an employee but not a customer as an advance return (`Dr cash / Cr employee advances`, `return` events FIFO across open advances, never beyond the unliquidated balance).
- Payment proposals (`/settlements`, direction `payment`) settle open payables in full for suppliers, or issue an employee advance when they carry no allocations; they never post. Beneficiary versions (`createBeneficiary`, `approveBeneficiary` by another principal) supersede the prior approved version and withdraw every payment authority bound to it (orders return to draft, approved proposals return to draft). Payment orders bind the proposal's content version at authorization; authority is refused to whoever prepared or submitted the proposal or the order; editing the proposal withdraws the authority; release (manual channel only; bank-file and API channels are P06) rechecks the binding and needs available evidence; settlement is the single financial effect (`Dr payables` or `Dr employee advances` / `Cr cash` / `Cr withholding payable`, allocations applied, advance issued) and is idempotent; return posts the mirrored entry, unwinds allocations and reverses the settlement's tax events.
- Withholding certificates derive from live header withholding events of a supplier, rule and period (`YYYY`, `YYYY-MM`, `YYYY-Qn`), credits negative, reversed events and their mirrors excluded; versions carry the output version and a content checksum, are reviewed and issued by principals other than the preparer and supersede earlier issued versions.
- Supplier aging reuses `sales.agingReport` with `side='AP'`.

Verification: `scripts/test-p05-domain.mjs` (10 groups) covers P05-T01 (bill 10,000 / 1,200 / 11,200 with 200 accrued withholding, supplier credit 2,000 net, payment 8,800 without repeated tax), P05-T02 (payment-time profile: 5,600 payable, settlement 5,500 cash + 100 withholding recognized once, repeated settlement inert, return reversing the event), P05-T03 (`si/0001` refused against `SI-0001`, distinct reference proceeds with the approver's disposition), P05-T04 (beneficiary change withdraws authority, release refused, one effect after re-authorization), P05-T05 (10,000 advance, 6,000 claim liquidation, 4,000 return closing the advance at a zero balance, claim beyond cover opening a payable), plus purchase orders with receipts of service and the two-way match, PO policy with non-PO accounts, certificates and AP aging; the P04 domain suite passes unchanged except that a receipt-direction check moved from `FEATURE_NOT_ENABLED` to `VALIDATION_FAILED`.

## P05-03 API and jobs

All 36 operations are served by `apps/api/src/workspace-api.mjs` with the shared conventions (X-Entity-Id, Idempotency-Key with request-hash receipts, If-Match on edits and actions, ETag on resources, typed errors). A document is addressable only through its family (`/bills` serves bills and supplier credits; a bill is 404 under `/purchase-orders` and `/expense-claims`; supplier credits never appear under `/invoices`). `POST /reports` accepts `reportType=ap_aging`, rendered by the worker through the same snapshot path as customer aging. The P06 `/bank-accounts` operations stay `FEATURE_NOT_ENABLED` and are now the later-phase probe in the P02 and P04 API suites.

Verification: `scripts/test-p05-api.mjs` (7 groups, API and worker as processes, in CI on fresh and upgraded databases): purchase order approval under separate roles, bill creation with server totals and contract rejection of client totals, idempotent replay, `DUPLICATE_SOURCE` for a normalized reference, two-way match refusal, ETag/If-Match, forbidden and stale approvals, versioned posting to `BILL-000001` opening the payable net of withholding, supplier credit correction, payment proposal and order through authority, `FEATURE_NOT_ENABLED` for bank-file release, mandatory evidence, settlement once under replay and repeated commands, return reopening the payable, expense claim to a payable, the supplier aging job and 404 isolation.

Verification: `tests/browser/workspace.spec.mjs` purchasing journey on the production composition — two-principal `purchasing` activation, supplier and control accounts on screen, purchase order through submit and approval, PO-linked bill with server totals through submit, approval and posting to `BILL-000001`, the order's billed column, payment proposal with a full allocation through submission, payment order, independent authority, manual release and bank settlement with evidence until the bill shows `paid`, supplier statement and aging job, WCAG checks on every purchasing route (also in CI). Workspace provisioning gained a `--treasury` principal.

## Open items for the owner

- The reviewed OpenAPI has no operations for the purchasing profile, bill numbering series, beneficiary versions, receipts of service, advances or withholding certificates. The domain implements them (`createBeneficiary`, `approveBeneficiary`, `listBeneficiaries`, `recordReceipt`, `listReceipts`, `purchaseOrderStatus`, `listAdvances`, `prepareCertificate`, `reviewCertificate`, `issueCertificate`, `listCertificates`) and the tests and journey seed or call them directly; the screens need read/list and versioned-mutation operations added to the contract before the beneficiary, receipt, advance and certificate screens can exist (the payment screen takes the approved beneficiary version id as text meanwhile).
- `DocumentResource` has no room for the bill's withholding amount or the PO match figures (`additionalProperties: false`); the bill screen shows the payable open item net of withholding and the orders screen derives the billed net from the bills list.
- Withholding assignment per supplier lives in `purchasing_profile.supplierWithholding` because `PartyCreate` has no withholding field; a party-level field would be the better home.
- `SettlementCreate.withholdingAmount` must equal the server-computed payment-time share; the screen cannot preview it without the bill withholding, so the error message states the expected amount.
- No seeded role template holds `purchase_order.create/edit/submit/cancel`; tenants create a purchaser role (tests and the journey do). Consider adding them to `clerk` or a `purchaser` template in a later seed migration.
- Bank-file and qualified-API release channels are refused until treasury (P06); `release_unknown` and `failed` states exist in the schema but no operation reaches them until a bank adapter reports.
- `ADR 001` still names NestJS/Vitest; the codebase remains plain Node + `node:test` (see `P02-REVIEW.md`).
